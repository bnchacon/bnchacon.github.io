02

Research

Gender

How do gender gaps begin at home?

Maternal employment and siblings' gender composition shape how households allocate unpaid work — reproducing gender gaps long before anyone enters the labor market.

Human capital

What do children inherit from their mothers' work?

Mothers' education and employment shape their children's school achievement, linking women's labor market position to the next generation's human capital.

Inequality and mobility

Where does opportunity break down in Mexico?

Migration, regional inequality and the quality of local government, with evidence disaggregated by state and metropolitan area.

Methods and data. Applied microeconometrics on Mexican national surveys (ENIGH, ENOE, ENUT) in STATA, Python and R. An ongoing project decomposes the gender gap in unpaid work across Mexico, Chile and Colombia, combining classical econometrics with causal machine learning.

03

Publications

2026

Effects of maternal employment and sibling’s gender on housework time allocation

With Ernesto Aguayo-Téllez Review of Economics of the Household · Under review, second round Working version (PDF)

Using the 2014, 2019 and 2024 rounds of Mexico’s Time Use Survey and a SUR model of all four household members, we estimate how housework is redistributed when the mother works. Daughters absorb more — especially alongside a brother — and trade it against paid work rather than study time. The pattern does not attenuate over the decade.
